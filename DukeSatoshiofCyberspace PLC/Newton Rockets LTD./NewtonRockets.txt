Newton Rockets is Britain’s vertically integrated space company: rockets, spacecraft, orbital infrastructure and space logistics in one business.

The strategic premise is simple: if Earth becomes covered by high-capacity satellite internet by 2030, space stops being a communications niche and becomes global infrastructure. The bottleneck moves from connectivity to getting hardware into orbit, replacing it, servicing it and eventually moving things around in space.

That is Newton's market.

The £100bn thesis

Newton isn't valued like a traditional rocket manufacturer.

It is valued as the European equivalent of an integrated space infrastructure company.

A £100bn Newton therefore needs to capture value across the whole chain:

Earth → Launch → Orbit → Satellite → Network → Orbital Services → Moon

Rocket launches are the entry point, not the entire business.

The model follows the lesson of companies such as Rocket Lab: launch becomes substantially more valuable when combined with spacecraft manufacturing, components and on-orbit services. Rocket Lab reported $602m of 2025 revenue, with space systems already larger than launch services.
