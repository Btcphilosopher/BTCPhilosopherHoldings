# YORK IMAGING
## SEE THE WORLD DIFFERENTLY.

### The world's next great imaging company will not be a camera company.

It will be an **imaging technology company**.

York Imaging exists to build the complete technological infrastructure for seeing, understanding and reproducing the physical world.

We combine:

**advanced sensors + optics + artificial intelligence + computational imaging + semiconductor technology + robotics + lighting + software.**

Our ambition is to build imaging systems **four years ahead of the commercial market**.

Not an incremental camera.

Not another electronics brand.

A new imaging platform.

---

# 01 — THE THESIS

The modern camera is approaching a technological convergence.

Sensor technology is becoming extraordinarily capable.

AI is transforming image processing.

Optics are increasingly computational.

Machine vision is becoming ubiquitous.

Lighting is becoming programmable.

Edge computing is becoming powerful enough to process enormous quantities of visual information locally.

Yet these technologies are still largely developed independently.

**York Imaging brings them together.**

We believe the next generation of imaging will be created by companies that control the entire pipeline:

**PHOTON → SENSOR → SILICON → AI → IMAGE → APPLICATION**

That is our company.

# 02 — THE YORK IMAGING ENGINE

Our core technology platform consists of seven layers.

### 1. SENSING

Next-generation image sensors designed for:

- extreme dynamic range
- high-speed capture
- low-light imaging
- high spectral sensitivity
- computational HDR
- global shutter
- scientific imaging
- machine vision

We will eventually design our own sensor architectures.

---

### 2. OPTICS

Advanced optical systems engineered alongside computational algorithms.

Instead of asking:

**"How perfect can we make the lens?"**

we ask:

**"How perfect can we make the entire optical system?"**

The lens and processor become one system.

---

### 3. COMPUTATIONAL IMAGING

Our cameras will not simply record light.

They will **model it**.

Our computational imaging engine will understand:

- light
- depth
- motion
- colour
- surfaces
- geometry
- atmospheric conditions
- lens behaviour
- sensor behaviour

The result is an entirely new class of image formation.

---

### 4. YORK AI

AI becomes part of the imaging architecture itself.

Not an AI chatbot bolted onto a camera.

Instead:

**AI inside the imaging pipeline.**

York AI will handle:

- autofocus prediction
- noise reconstruction
- exposure
- colour science
- object tracking
- depth estimation
- image reconstruction
- optical correction
- motion compensation
- scene understanding

And increasingly:

**real-time visual intelligence.**

---

### 5. YORK SILICON

Long-term, we develop our own imaging processors.

Purpose-built silicon for:

**visual computation.**

Dedicated acceleration for:

- neural imaging
- sensor processing
- computer vision
- optical correction
- HDR
- autofocus
- video
- edge AI

This gives York Imaging control over the computational heart of its products.

---

### 6. INTELLIGENT LIGHTING

Lighting becomes part of the imaging computer.

York lighting systems communicate directly with the camera.

The system understands:

**what is being photographed + how it is being illuminated + how the sensor responds.**

Lighting therefore becomes programmable.

A studio becomes a computational environment.

A film set becomes a real-time imaging system.

A laboratory becomes an optical computing environment.

---

### 7. IMAGING SOFTWARE

All York hardware runs on a common imaging platform.

**York Imaging OS**

provides the software architecture connecting:

- cameras
- sensors
- lenses
- lighting
- AI
- storage
- displays
- cloud systems
- professional workflows
- industrial imaging systems

The objective is a single imaging ecosystem.

Our internal rule:

> **If the technology can be commercialised today, it is probably not ambitious enough.**

Our R&D organisation is divided into:

### NOW

### +2

Technologies moving towards commercialisation.

### +4

Technologies that define the next generation.

### +8
